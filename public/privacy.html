🍣 Sushi Empire — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-27
Short version: there is no account and no server. Your game save lives only in your browser or device's local storage. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data.

What data the game stores

Your entire game progress (currency, levels, purchases, settings) is saved locally on your own device using localStorage. It is never sent to us or to any server we control — we don't have one. You can export or delete this save at any time from the in-game Settings screen.

Advertising

The mobile version (Android/iOS) uses Google AdMob to show optional, rewarded video ads that give in-game rewards. Ads are never forced. Where required (e.g. the EU/EEA), Google's User Messaging Platform asks for your consent to personalized ads before any ad loads — you can decline and still see the same number of ads, just less targeted. See Google's advertising privacy policy for what AdMob itself collects.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play / the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Purchases can be restored from the Shop screen if you reinstall or switch devices.

Analytics

We use aggregate, anonymous gameplay analytics (e.g. which screens are used, session length) to understand what's working and fix what isn't. This data is not linked to your identity and is not sold.

Children

Sushi Empire is a general-audience game and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change. Significant changes will be noted in the app's update notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
